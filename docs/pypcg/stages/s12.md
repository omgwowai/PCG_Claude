# Stage 12 -- Rooftops (`PCG_3_3_2_Rooftops`, 屋顶设备)

Python: `Content/Python/pypcg/stages/s12_rooftops.py` (MAIN: the class / size / height split and the two offsets),
`s12_rooftops_assemblies.py` (the WFC points less the small assemblies' points); shared helpers in
`pypcg/geo/frames.py` (`SplinePolygon`, new) and `pypcg/geo/ground.py` (`difference_binary` / `volume_overlap`:
steepness, the engine's overlap ratio, a broad phase); graph: `pypcg/graphs/s12_rooftops.py`; checks:
`pypcg/checks/s12.py`; tests: `Tools/pypcg/tests/test_s12_rooftops.py`. Measured on the demo level, 2026-10-20,
against the official graph at component seed 42 (`Tools/pypcg/data/s12_official_measurements.json`,
`s12_fixture.json`; generators `Content/Python/pypcg_s12_measure.py` and `Tools/pypcg/s12_measure_offline.py`).

## 2. The computation

Read from the official IR (`Tools/pypcg/official_ir/PCG_3_3_2_Rooftops.json`) and the primitives' own graphs
(`Filter_Areas_BySize` and its CoreProcess in `Saved/graph_dumps/t20/`; `Transform_Offset`, `Compose_Points_Difference`,
`Fill_Areas_WithFittingAssets_with_Corrective` and their CoreProcesses in `Saved/t18/ir/`) and the engine source
(`PCGDifferenceElement.cpp`, `PCGDifferenceData.cpp`, `PCGBasePointData.cpp`, `PCGTransformPoints.cpp`). Names in
brackets are the official nodes each step replaces.

1. **Class** -- `FilterDataByTag_0 / _2 / _3` (`SelectedTags` SmallRooftops / MediumRooftops / LargeRooftops, exact).
2. **Size** -- `Subgraph_9 / Subgraph_6 / Subgraph_5 Filter_Areas_BySize` (Size 300 / 800 / 500, Operator Greater).
   Inside: `InputsProcessing_ForcePolygon` (`CreatePolygon2D` of each closed spline), then `@data.$area` > Size x 1e4
   (`AttributeMathsOp_0` Multiply, `FilterDataByAttribute_3`), tagged Selected / Unselected. The polygon is
   `geo.frames.SplinePolygon`: the spline's polyline (`ConvertSplineToPolyLine`, closing point popped), Newell's frame,
   the stored local ring, its area; the ToPoint vertices as `GetTransformAtDistance(i, 0)` reads them.
3. **Height** -- `Reroute_0` (the large ones above 500 m2), `AttributeReduce_0` on `$position.Z` with its DEFAULT
   operation, **Average** (probed: the CDO's `operation` is `AVERAGE`; the official node sets none) although the node is
   titled "Reduce Z To Max Height Per Building": the vertices' z summed in order times `float(1 / n)` (traps.md 72).
   `CopyAttributes_0` puts it on `@data.height` (each source to its target), `FilterDataByAttribute_1`
   (`@data.height`, FilterByValue, the default operator Greater -- probed --, constant 7000) splits Inside / Outside.
   The value is the roof's WORLD z (the demo terrain itself lies some 20 m up: trap 52 measures 2 536 cm at the
   origin, and the lowest roof here is at 2 113 cm), so "Height > 70m" is the roof above 70 m, not the building 70 m
   tall. On these flat roofs the average equals every vertex's z to the last bit.
4. **Offsets** -- `Subgraph_8` (-8 cm) and `Subgraph_10` (-20 cm) `Transform_Offset` (PerPoints true, non-absolute).
   Its CoreProcess splits by type: a POLYGON goes `GetSplineControlPoints_5` (its ToPoint vertices) ->
   `TransformPoints_2` (location + `FTransform(rotation).TransformPosition(offset)`: each vertex's own rotation applies)
   -> `CreatePolygon2D_0` on those points, a NEW polygon; a SPLINE goes `GetSplineControlPoints_1` ->
   `TransformPoints_1` -> `AttributeMathsOp_13` (moved - old) -> `OffsetSpline_0`: control points moved, the rest kept.
   Output order: the polygons, then the splines (`DefaultOutputNode`'s edge order). The port: `offset_polygon` moves the
   ToPoint vertices by `quat_rotate(rotation, (0, 0, dz))` and keeps each vertex's own z (`Polygon.zs`, new) --
   Newell's normal on a flat ring is a hair off vertical (the sums of coordinate differences are not exactly 0), so
   the vertices differ in the last bit of z and so does the offset; MakePolygon2DData then builds the polygon from the
   same 3D points `CreatePolygon2D_0` does. `offset_spline`: control points z + dz.
5. **The four fitting nodes' inputs** (`OUT` in the module):
   - `Subgraph_2` <- `Subgraph_8`: the large > 500, the large <= 500, the medium > 800 (offset polygons), then every
     small rooftop (offset splines) -- `Subgraph_8`'s PrimaryInput order;
   - `Subgraph_7` <- the large <= 500, the small > 300, the large > 500 at <= 7000 cm;
   - `Subgraph_3` <- `Subgraph_10`: the large > 500 above 7000 cm (offset polygons);
   - `Subgraph_11` <- the medium <= 800, the small <= 300.
   The rooftops of `Subgraph_7` and `Subgraph_11` are only filtered, so the port FORWARDS each as the very spline it
   came in as (`FORWARD_SPLINES`, stage 11's opt-in): the fitting node's own `InputsProcessing_ForcePolygon` runs the
   same `CreatePolygon2D` on the same spline and builds the official polygon itself (6.2, `wfc_inputs` / `vent_inputs`
   126 / 80 equal).
6. **The difference** (`s12_rooftops_assemblies`) -- `Subgraph_12 Compose_Points_Difference` "Subtract Small
   Assemblies From WFC Points": Primary `Subgraph_7`'s points (tagged `Input:0`), Secondary `Subgraph_2`'s
   (`Input:1`), `FilterDataByType` points, then `Difference_0` (Mode **Discrete**, DensityFunction **Binary**,
   bDiffMetadata false) and `ToPoint_0`. Per the engine: each Source item on its own, against every Differences item
   at once (`AddDifference`, a union); a source point is sampled with its transform and its plain bounds
   (`SampleBasedRangeProcessing`: `GetLocalBounds`), each difference point counts with its DENSITY box -- bounds x
   (2 - steepness), x 1.5 here, every fitting point carrying steepness 0.5 -- through `VolumeOverlap` both ways
   (`ComputeOverlapRatio`: per axis, an axis where the denominator has no extent counts 1); Binary: any overlapping
   difference point of density > 0 drops the source point, and a source point is also dropped when its own density is
   not above 0 (`Density = source - sampled > 0`). A kept point is the source point as it was (`SetFromValueRanges`).
   No difference data at all: the sources pass through. `Primitives_PrepareOutputs` drops empty results.
   -> `Subgraph_13`, first of `Branch_1`'s In (`Subgraph_12, 11, 3, 2`).

Surface: 22 official nodes; the 13 computation nodes are in Python (the reroute as an edge), the 5 generation nodes
kept, 3 dropped with `Partitioned`, plus the scene read.

## 3. Deliberate differences

1. **Forwarded splines instead of polygons into `Subgraph_7` / `Subgraph_11`.** Officially they receive the polygons
   `Filter_Areas_BySize` made; the port hands over the splines those polygons were made from, and the fitting node's
   `InputsProcessing_ForcePolygon` makes the identical polygon (same `CreatePolygon2D`, same spline object). Inside the
   fitting node converted splines come before polygons (`ForcePolygon`'s `DeleteTags_0` order), but each of these two
   inputs is one kind only, so the order is the official one (6.2: every fitting output equal).
2. **The offset splines carry identity control-point rotations.** `OffsetSpline_0` keeps the building node's
   rotations; `convert` cannot read them (traps.md 63), so the written splines have none. Their only reader,
   `CreatePolygon2D` in `Subgraph_2`, uses positions alone (6.2: `Subgraph_2` equal on every point).
3. **Kept points keep their engine quaternions** (`EXACT_ROTATIONS`, new opt-in read by `host.run`): the point writer
   otherwise rebuilds each rotation from its rotator, which does not give the same bits. Found on the way:
   `unreal.Quat(x, y, z, w)` rounds every component to float32; the writer assigns `q.x ...` instead (probed).
4. **No partition**: `Branch_1`, `GetGraphParameter_3`, `Loop_0` dropped.
5. **Tags**: the offset polygons carry the input tags less `Type:*`, plus `Type:Polygon2D` and Selected / Unselected;
   the forwarded splines their input tags plus Selected / Unselected; the difference output `Type:Point`,
   `Primitive:ComposePointsDifference`. The fitting nodes route by data type and keep no input tag.

## 4. What could not be reproduced, and what was done instead

Nothing measured differs (6.2). Two shared helpers were extended rather than copied:

1. `geo.ground.volume_overlap` / `difference_binary` (stage 13's): the diff point's density box (steepness), the
   second ratio over the TRANSFORMED query box, `ComputeOverlapRatio`'s per-axis rule, `drop_empty_sources`. Stage 13
   passes no steepness (1: unchanged) and leaves `drop_empty_sources` off (its old behaviour); its boxes have extent on
   every axis, so the ratio rule does not move its result. Its 6-tuple rows still work.
2. `difference_binary` now finds the pairs from conservative world boxes (grown 0.01 cm) on a grid and tests each with
   the exact `VolumeOverlap`; the first form (a fixed reach around every source, called once per WFC item) took 109 s
   on 9 137 x 19 134 points, now 0.5 s (Step F: no native fallback needed). Tested against the pairwise brute force.

## 5. Corrections to the brief

1. **"the Average (not max) Z reduction"**: confirmed -- `AttributeReduce_0` sets no operation and the CDO default is
   Average; the code averages (`average_z`, `sum x float(1 / n)`), the test `test_the_height_split_is_the_average_z_
   not_the_max` puts a tilted roof whose max is above 70 m and average below it on the WFC side. On this level every
   rooftop is flat, so average = max = every vertex's z; the nearest roof is 28.6 cm from 7000.
2. **"small / WFC / skyscraper sets do not overlap"** is not what the official graph does for small vs skyscraper.
   As rooftops: the small-assembly node gets every rooftop but the medium <= 800 m2 (so it shares rooftops with the WFC
   and skyscraper nodes by design); the WFC, skyscraper and vent rooftops are disjoint. As meshes: only WFC vs small is
   subtracted (`Subgraph_12`); officially 1 074 of the 1 604 skyscraper points overlap a small-assembly point
   (`VolumeOverlap` > 0, measured on the official taps) and stay. After the difference no WFC point overlaps a small
   or a skyscraper one (0 of 5 765 each), no skyscraper point a kept WFC one, and no vent point a small or kept WFC one
   (0 of 1 158). The test and the check assert the rooftop disjointness and the WFC-vs-small rule.
3. **"Partitioned goes"**: yes (1).
4. **"Python: ... the small downward offset"**: two offsets, -8 cm (small assemblies) and -20 cm (skyscraper ones).

## 7. Carry

- `Polygon.zs` (per-vertex z) and `EXACT_ROTATIONS` (points written with their engine quaternions) are available to
  later stages; both are off by default and change nothing for the stages already ported.
- `geo.ground.difference_binary` now takes steepness (7th row element) and `drop_empty_sources`, and is fast enough for
  tens of thousands of points; stage 13 calls it as before. Its `drop_empty_sources` is OFF there although the engine
  drops density-0 source points whenever there are differences: stage 13's birch points under a projector miss have
  density 0 and would go (not measured here; s13.md 4.5 counted 515 = 515 birches left).
- `unreal.Quat(x, y, z, w)` rounds to float32 (traps.md 73): `convert._ue("Quat", ...)`, the writer of Quat-typed
  attributes, still uses it.
