# Stage 10 -- Ground (`PCG_3_2_1_Ground`, 地面与人行道)

Python: `Content/Python/pypcg/stages/s10_ground.py` (helpers `pypcg/geo/ground.py`, `pypcg/geo/areas.py`);
graph: `pypcg/graphs/s10_ground.py`; checks: `pypcg/checks/s10.py`; tests:
`Tools/pypcg/tests/test_s10_ground.py`. Measured against the official graph on the demo level's stage 10
volume (seed 42, 2026-10-19), through a tap graph of the official nodes (`pyg_probe_t17_tap.py`) and the
`Assign_Assets` oracle.

## 1. Contract

| | |
|---|---|
| Inputs | `Lots` (`Subgraph_0 Get Lots`: the stage-9 volume, every tag; the stage reads `Lots` and `reservedLots`), `Districts` (`Subgraph_2`; reads `UndividableDistricts`), `City` (`Subgraph_18`, actor tag City), `MainTower` (`Subgraph_50 Get Landmark`), `LandmarkPark` (`Subgraph_43`), `CentralParkFootprint` (`Subgraph_51`), `MeshTerrain` (`Subgraph_39 Get_MeshTerrain`, Final, a surface), `ArteryWidth` (a param: `Subgraph_21 Get Roads` -> `FilterDataByTag_4` -> `ExtractAttribute_1`, kept native, see 2.4). `Subgraph_44 Get Base Terrain` is kept and left unwired: the official graph has no consumer for it either |

| Generation | `Subgraph_26` Assign Corners, `Subgraph_31` Assign Sidewalks, `Subgraph_20` Assign Railing And Fence Grammar, `Subgraph_32` Spawn ground tiles, `Subgraph_35` Spawn Sidewalk Assets, `Subgraph_36` Spawn Fence Assets -- kept, with the native nodes BETWEEN them (2.5) |

## 2. The computation

### 2.1 Ground tiles (`GroundTiles`)

1. **The buildable pieces** -- `Subgraph_1/16 Filter_Tags` (exact tag match) pick `Lots` and `reservedLots`,
   `Subgraph_5` the `UndividableDistricts`; `Subgraph_7 Compose_Areas_Exclusion` takes each lot less the
   union of the undividable districts and the small landmark park, `Subgraph_22` less the reserved lots
   (`geo.areas.exclusion`: one input polygon at a time, every result polygon its own item, no sliver
   filter). 129 pieces on the demo level (official 107, see 4.1).
2. **Grown 150 cm** -- `Subgraph_10 Transform_Areas_Expand` (`Expand` 150): `OffsetPolygon2D` per piece,
   holes included, and NO union -- `UnionBeforeOutputting` is false by default on this primitive (its user
   parameter; `Transform_Areas_Contract`'s is true). Neighbouring pieces overlap and both tile the overlap,
   as officially. `geo.areas.expand`.
3. **The 3 m grid** -- `Subgraph_9 Fill_Areas_Uniformly` (`PointExtents` 150), read node by node from
   `Fill_Areas_Uniformly_CoreProcess` (53 nodes) and the engine (`geo.ground.fill_uniform`):
   - the frame is the control point at the start of the polygon's FIRST longest outer segment
     (`@Data.$LongestOuterSegmentIndex`, `PCGPolygon2DAccessor.h:253`): a yaw-only rotation
     `MakeFromXZ(direction, Up).ToQuat()`;
   - every vertex goes into that frame with `InverseTransformDirection` -- a rotation about the WORLD
     origin, no translation -- and `CreateSurfaceFromSpline` -> `SurfaceSampler_0` (`Looseness` 0,
     `bUnbounded`) samples the integer lattice `(i x 300, j x 300)` over `CeilToInt((min - 1)/300) ..
     FloorToInt((max + 1)/300)` (the spline bounds grow by the points' scale, 1 cm), keeping a cell when
     `FPolygon2d::Contains` -- the winding number of the crossings strictly to its right, half-open in Y --
     puts it inside (`PCGSurfaceSampler.cpp:117-120, 276-296`, `PCGSplineInteriorSurfaceData.cpp:202`);
   - back to the world with `TransformDirection`; `$Rotation` = the frame, roll zeroed; the sampler's box
     +-150 on all three axes; steepness 1; z = the piece's (the flat interior surface's height).
   Measured on the 107 official pieces: the same count in every piece (155 859), locations within
   3.1e-9 cm (11 689 bit for bit), yaw within 2.8e-12 deg, every box and steepness exact.
4. **Lifted 15 cm** -- `Subgraph_33 Transform_Offset` (`offset` (0, 0, 15), per point, non-absolute): z + 15.
5. **Fitted to the terrain** -- `Subgraph_40 Custom_Projector_with_rotation_fix` (32 nodes, defaults
   `ProjectPosition`, `ProjectRotation`, `RestoreOrigZPos` on, `Z_offset_for_proj` 0; `geo.ground.project_tile`):
   the point moved to three corners of its box (`ResetPointCenter_0/1/2`: (min, min), (max, min), (min, max)
   at mid height, rotated by the point's own rotation), each dropped onto the surface (`Projection_1/2/3`),
   `X = unit(P10 - P00)`, `Y = unit(P01 - P00)`, `MakeRotFromXY(X, Y)` -> `$Rotation`; the point itself
   dropped onto the surface (`Projection_0`) and `$Position.Z += StoredPostion.Z`. The surface's
   `ProjectPoint` is `UPCGMeshPartitionData`'s fallback to `SamplePoint`, a vertical ray -- exactly the
   call `pypcg.ue.terrain.Terrain.height` makes. Measured on 20 000 official tiles (fed the official
   fill): location bit for bit on all 20 000, the quaternion within 1.3e-9 (the rotator <= 1.7e-7 deg;
   the official tilts reach 21 deg). 4 surface samples per tile, 10.8 s of the stage's 15 s.
6. **Seven ways** -- `FC_RandomChoice_1..6` (`Ratio` 1/7, 1/6, 1/5, 1/4, 1/3, 1/2; `RandomPerDataset`, the
   primitive's default): one draw per remaining PIECE at each step (`geo.keep_each`), the sixth step's
   Unselected the seventh way. Whole pieces get one tile mesh, so the per-mesh shares swing with the
   piece sizes (official A..G: 28 031 .. 30 369 .. 17 305). Python's own draws (spec E2).
7. **One plaza tile per way** -- `AssignGroundTiles_A..G` (`Assets` = one `SM_Plaza_Square_X` static mesh,
   `PositionOffset` (-150, -150, 0), everything else default): `geo.assets.expand_items` -- each call merges
   its pieces' points (`$Seed += dataIndex`), and since the asset is a static mesh the output is the moved
   targets alone: the tile corner `loc + RotateVector(q, (-150, -150, 0))`, the mesh's box
   (`UseMeshBounds`), `Asset` = the mesh. Measured by the oracle (section 6.4): every field of all
   155 859 points bit for bit, on all seven calls.
8. **Gathered** in `Gather_0`'s order (A, B, C, D, E, G, F) into `Subgraph_32 Spawn ground tiles`.

### 2.2 Sidewalk segments (`SidewalkSegments`)

1. **The pedestrian boundary** -- `ExtractAttribute_1` (`@data.ArteryWidth` of the Roads item whose tags
   contain `Polygon`, i.e. the stage-7 `RoadFootprints`: 3400) and `AttributeMathsOp_1` (/ 2) give the
   `Expand` of `Subgraph_23 Transform_Areas_Expand` on the City outline (a closed spline, read through its
   1 cm polyline as stage 9 reads it). The grown polygon's vertices (`FilterDataByType_0`,
   `GetSplineControlPoints_0`) become a closed linear spline (`CreateSpline_0`), reversed
   (`Subgraph_3 Transform_Splines_Reverse`) so it runs clockwise.
   **The grow runs in the polygon's own frame** (`geo.areas.in_frame`: yaw -90 about the vertex mean, the
   frame `CreatePolygon2D` gives every polygon -- probed on every Lots, district and road polygon). Only the
   start vertex of Clipper's result depends on it, but the cut below splits the loop at its start: computed
   in the world the ring started 754 vertices later than the official one; in the frame it starts on the
   official vertex (1.1e-10 cm).
2. **Cut at the central park** -- `Subgraph_52` grows the central park 19 m, `Subgraph_49 Compose_Spline_Cut`
   (`ClipPaths(Difference)`) removes the part of the loop inside it: `geo.clip_path` on the loop as an open
   path from its start, so a piece never runs across the start (the engine's open-path clip does not
   either). Two pieces on the demo level (583 and 437 vertices), handed back LAST FIRST as `ClipPaths`
   does here.
3. **Cut at the corners** -- `Branch_1 Output A` = the boundary pieces then the Lots; `Subgraph_8
   Extract_Segments` (`CutAngleThreshold` 20) splits each at every control point turning more than 20
   degrees (`geo.extract_segments` for the closed lots, `geo.ground.extract_open_segments` for an open
   piece, whose two ends always close a run).
4. **The landmark** -- `Subgraph_46 Transform_Areas_Contract` (300, in the polygon's frame, for the same
   start-vertex reason), `Subgraph_4 Extract_Segments` (5 degrees).
   Measured: all 469 segments in the official order, 315 bit for bit, the rest within 2.9e-11 cm, except the
   two boundary pieces' cut ends (<= 0.0033 cm, 4.2).

### 2.3 Corner shapes (`CornerShapes`)

`Subgraph_26 Assign Corners` reads `Branch_1 Output A` (the boundary pieces, then the Lots) and
`Subgraph_46` (the contracted landmark). The boundary pieces must carry their **control points' own
rotations**: the corner grammar measures the turn at each control point from them, and with identity
rotations it put NO corner on the two pieces (112 of the official 1 228 corners missing, every one of them
on the boundary; measured by instance position). Each vertex keeps the yaw `GetSplineControlPoints_0`
gave it -- the direction of its outgoing edge on the counter-clockwise ring -- through `CreateSpline_0` and
the reverse (`PCGSplineDirection.cpp:85`, rotations carried unchanged), and a cut point takes
`SplitSpline`'s `GetQuaternionAtSplineInputKey`, the direction of travel (`PCGSplitSplines.cpp:107`). With
them: all six corner meshes equal the official (355 / 136 / 177 / 330 / 119 / 111), every instance within
0.0046 cm. The lot polygons and the landmark polygon pass as polygons, as officially (118 shapes, the
official order).

### 2.4 Railing zones (`RailingZones`)

`Subgraph_14 Compose_Areas_Intersection` (each lot x the undividable districts), `Subgraph_29
Compose_Areas_Exclusion` (less the landmark), `Subgraph_19 Transform_Offset` (+21 cm: on a polygon the
CoreProcess moves its control points, `GetSplineControlPoints_5` -> `TransformPoints_2` ->
`CreatePolygon2D_0`) -> the kept `Subgraph_20`. Measured: both zones within 5.8e-11 cm, z 21.

### 2.5 What stays native -- a DEVIATION from the brief (ruling T17a, accepted)

19 of the brief's 76 computation nodes stay native:

- **16 computation nodes on the generated grammar points**: the three post-grammar projectors
  (`Subgraph_34/38/48`), the street-prop roll/pitch fix (`AttributeCast_0`, `FilterDataByAttribute_0..3`,
  `AttributeMathsOp_0/3`) and the transparent-vehicle duplicate (`FilterDataByAttribute_4`, `FilterDataByType_1`,
  `AttributeStringOp_0/1`, `CopyAttributes_1`, `AttributeCast_1`);
- **1 gather** (`Gather_1`);
- **2 read adapters** (`FilterDataByTag_4`, `ExtractAttribute_1`): a Python node cannot read a data-domain
  attribute in this build (`PCGMetadataDomainHandle` has no reader in Python; probed), so the artery width
  comes in as a param.

Of the 57 counted as Python, 20 are named reroutes (`NamedRerouteDeclaration` x4, `NamedRerouteUsage` x16)
that became plain edges, so the real computation in Python is about **37 nodes**. This is accepted on the
stage-9 precedent (its post-grammar projector stayed native) under the user's goal of "most" computation in
Python.

**Why, correctly stated.** A terrain input is NOT the blocker -- `Subgraph_40` is ported in this same stage
with the surface on a Python pin. The real costs are (1) carrying the grammar points' full attribute set
through a second Python node behind each grammar node (`SlimGraph(more_modules=...)`; `convert` would have
to round-trip every attribute the spawners and the vehicle chain read, `Asset` and the grammar's own), and
(2) a `PyPCGTools` C++ wrapper that reads `@data` attributes, so the two read adapters can go too. Both are
for the wrap-up to schedule.

## 3. Deliberate differences

1. **The seven-way split** draws Python's own numbers (spec E2): which piece gets which tile differs from
   the official seed's, so the per-mesh tile shares differ per seed; the tile total does not (every tile
   is assigned).
2. **No debug branches, no partition** (`Branch_0..7`, `Gather_2`, `Subgraph_12 GridPartition`, `Loop_0`).
3. **The sidewalk segments carry no control-point rotations** (the official ones do): the sidewalk grammar
   does not read them -- every sidewalk, curb, prop and vehicle mesh count is equal to the official without
   them -- so they are left out rather than modelled.
4. **A missing artery width** grows the City by the primitive's default `Expand` (100 cm), which is what the
   official graph does with the pin unfed.

## 4. What could not be reproduced

1. **Zero-area slivers in the buildable pieces (+338 tiles, +0.22 %).** The total area of the pieces is the
   official's to 1e-9 m2, and every non-degenerate piece matches; but where a reserved lot shares an edge
   with its own lot, the subtraction leaves degenerate slivers, and the 150 cm growth turns each into a
   300 cm band of tiles. The official graph has 107 pieces, 12 of them degenerate (247 tiles); the port
   (booleans in world coordinates) 129, 34 degenerate (585 tiles). Fed the official pieces, the port's fill
   gives the official 155 859 exactly. Two frame experiments, both measured on the demo level's inputs:
   - all booleans in the Lots' shared polygon transform (`get_transform()`: every Lots polygon carries the
     same one, the City's): 130 pieces;
   - **per subject in its own `CreatePolygon2D` frame** (vertex mean, yaw -90, the frame `ops.cut_with_paths`
     uses; subject and clips moved into it, both exclusion steps; ruling T17b): **130 pieces, 35 degenerate,
     156 164 tiles** -- not the official 107, so NOT adopted (`pypcg_s10_measure.py {"step": "frame"}`,
     `Saved/Reports/v2/s10/frame_exclusion.json`).
   The slivers are last-bit noise in the INPUTS: the official reserved lots were cut in stage 9 by a different
   sequence of booleans from the polygons' stored LOCAL coordinates, which the port only sees as world
   coordinates (`Polygon2DToJson`). Reproducing them needs those local coordinates and the engine's
   `GetRelativeTransform` arithmetic. **Limit for stages 13 and 18:** they reuse `A.exclusion` on geometry
   they PUBLISH, where trap 62 bites -- expect last-bit differences, and sliver pieces of different count,
   wherever their subjects share edges with their clips; measure downstream per mesh, do not assume equality.
2. **The two park-cut ends of the boundary (<= 0.0033 cm).** `SplitSpline` places a cut end through the
   spline's float input key (`FindInputKeyClosestToWorldLocation`); the port interpolates exactly. Every
   instance built on those pieces is equal.

## 5. Corrections to the brief

1. **"The width arrives as the Polygon tag"**: the official node picks the Roads item by a tag CONTAINING
   `Polygon` (`FilterDataByTag_4`, `Substring`) -- the stage-7 footprint's `Type:Polygon2D` -- and reads its
   `@data.ArteryWidth` (3400); the node title's "Has ArteryWidth Attr" is wrong about what it filters on.
2. **"No tile point is inside a building footprint"** holds for the landmark (`MainTower`) only: the official
   graph tiles the whole buildable lot, and the stage-9 building footprints are pieces of those lots, so
   tiles lie under them by design. The check asserts the landmark.
3. **"The tile points lie outside the excluded areas"**: the 150 cm growth reaches into them; the check
   asserts that no tile centre is deeper than 150 cm inside one.
4. **"Every sidewalk segment runs along a lot edge"**: the boundary pieces run along the grown City
   outline and the landmark's along its contracted ring; the check matches each segment to its own source.
5. **"The seven tile assets are all used across a run"** is a statement about a city-sized run (one draw
   per piece: 7 (6/7)^129 = 1.7e-8 on the demo level); the check asserts it from 100 pieces up.
6. **"76 in Python"** is not met: 57 are (about 37 real computation, 20 being reroutes), 19 stay native. This is
   a deviation accepted under ruling T17a, not a correction of the brief; see 2.5.

## 6. Results (2026-10-19)

`Content/Python/pypcg_s10_measure.py`, distiller `Tools/pypcg/s10_measurements.py`).

2. **Instances**: official 220 128 on four reads (two generations, plus a re-read; identical per mesh to the
   stage-9 session's record); slim 220 466 on all five seeds. Every one of the 53 non-tile meshes (sidewalks,
   curbs, corners, props, vehicles, fences: 64 269) equal per mesh on every seed; tiles 156 197 against
   155 859 (+0.22 %, 4.1), the seven tile meshes' shares by the seed's split.
3. **V2**: pass. With no output tags it compares the instance total and every mesh over 1 % share (15 rows):
   total +0.15 % (limit 25 %), the largest share move -3.2 pp (limit 10 pp, a tile mesh). Determinism:
   official and official_after identical.
   **What V2 cannot see here:** no positions at all; 45 of the 53 non-tile meshes sit under the 1 % share
   floor and are not compared; and its "determinism" compares the official graph against itself (trap 60), so
   it says nothing about the seeded port. The evidence that carries the result is the per-mesh equality of all
   53 non-tile meshes on every seed, the tap and oracle measurements -- all in
   `Tools/pypcg/data/s10_official_measurements.json`.
4. **V1**: 5 runs, `replayed` 0 (a surface input, trap 60), `deterministic` true, failures none. Each record
   is checked by `checks/s10.check_record`: a partial replay on a FLAT surface (all non-tile values field for

   does not move (measured bit for bit), not the recorded run's. The tile and segment counts are consistency
   checks (rebuilt with the stage's own functions). Shown to bite on real data: the landmark moved onto the
   largest ground piece flags 5 456 tiles.
5. **Assign oracle**: the seven `AssignGroundTiles_*` calls bit for bit on every field (`assign_oracle_rows.json`,
   run `s10_ground_tiles_shipped`).
6. **Timing**: 14.9 .. 15.1 s per seed (10.8 s of it the terrain fit), under the 60 s of Step F.
