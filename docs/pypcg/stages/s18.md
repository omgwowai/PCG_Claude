Python: `Content/Python/pypcg/stages/s18_outer_forest.py` (the footprint, the exclusion areas and the scatter) and
`s18_outer_forest_trees.py` (everything after the two texture writes); shared helpers `pypcg/geo/ground.py`
(`fill_random`, now banded), `pypcg/geo/distance.py` (new: `Write_DistanceFrom` on points) and
`pypcg/geo/assets.py` (`merge_targets`, `index_picks`, `expand_items`); graph: `pypcg/graphs/s18_outer_forest.py`;
checks: `pypcg/checks/s18.py`; tests: `Tools/pypcg/tests/test_s18_outer_forest.py` (fixture
`Tools/pypcg/data/s18_fixture.json`). Measured on the demo level's stage-18 volume, component seed 42, 2026-10-20:
`/Game/PyPCG/Probe/PCG_T23TapA` (the official graph, 17 taps) and `PCG_T23TapB` (the official graph with the
substitute texture, 19 taps), built and read by `Content/Python/pyg_probe_t23_tap.py` into `Saved/t23/`.

## 0. The texture, confirmed (trap 66)

`/Game/Textures/Noise/T_CloudNoise_4k` does not exist in this project (`EditorAssetLibrary.does_asset_exist` false;
`/Game/Textures/Noise` holds only `T_Noises_Clouds_4k_` and `T_Noises_Grunge_4k_`). A fresh official generation
logs `GetTextureData_0 ... could not be loaded` twice (one per texture write), then `No Valid Input Data Found`,
`Missing Primary Input Data To Assign Assets on` and `... To Spawn Assets on`; its census is **0 instances**, as
the three older records on disk (`Saved/Reports/v2/s05|s07|s08/down_18_official.json`). Tap A shows where it
breaks: 308 785 scattered points reach `Subgraph_12`, nothing leaves it (`t13`, `t14` have no item).

**The texture writes sit directly after the scatter.** `Subgraph_12` reads `FillRandomly_0`; the projection, the
water split and both distance steps are all downstream of it. So on the demo level the official graph computes
only the footprint, the exclusion areas and the scatter; everything after is empty, officially and in the port.
The brief's list of steps "upstream of the texture read" (the areas, the scatter, the projection and the
water / shore / deep-forest decision) is wrong for the last two (section 5). Those steps were therefore measured
on an ORACLE: the official graph with the substitute texture `T_Noises_Clouds_4k_` set on both writes (tap B,
never a shipped graph), which places 34 659 trees.

**What this does to verification.** V2's instance comparison on the demo level is 0 against 0 and proves only
that both graphs place nothing. The weight falls on: the per-step comparison against the two tap graphs (section
6, every deterministic step bit for bit), V1's partial replay of the first node (section 7), and an end-to-end
oracle run of the slim graph with the same substitute texture (section 7).

## 2. The computation

Polygon booleans are `pypcg.ue.ops` (Clipper2 through GeometryScript); `geo.areas` gives the engine's
one-subject-at-a-time semantics.

### 2.1 `s18_outer_forest` (up to the scatter)

1. **The footprint** -- `Subgraph_8 Extract_Bounds` (`CombineAllInputsIntoSinglePoint`: `MergePoints_0`,
   `CombinePoints_1`): the union of every terrain point's OWN box (`FBox::TransformBy` of its bounds by its
   transform -- the points are tilted, so the box reaches 149 415.78 cm where the points stop at 149 414.06),
   `bCenterPivot` re-centring it; then `Subgraph_9 Trace_Bound_Footprint` (`CenterPlaneZ`): four
   `ResetPointCenter` corners at the box's middle height, `CreateSpline_0` closed linear, `ForceCounterClockwise`.
   `footprint()` transforms only the points that can reach an extreme (within 2 sqrt(3) x the largest half-size of
   it) and repeats the two `ResetPointCenter` lerps: all four corners bit for bit (6.1).
2. **The city exclusion** -- `ExtractAttributeAtIndex_0` (native) gives `ArteryWidth`; `AttributeMathsOp_1`
   halves it, `AttributeMathsOp_CityBuffer` adds `CityEdgeBuffer` (1 700 + 100 = 1 800 cm on the demo, tapped);
   `Subgraph_3 Transform_Areas_Expand` grows the City outline by that, in the outline's own polygon frame
   (`geo.areas.in_frame`, stage 10's reading); no road footprint leaves the pin empty and `Subgraph_3`'s own
   `Expand` override (3 400) applies.
3. **Less the city** -- `ExcludeCity_0 Compose_Areas_Exclusion`: the footprint less the grown city.
4. **Less the park and the highway** -- `Subgraph_5` grows the park footprint by `ParkBuffer`; `Subgraph_1
   Compose_Areas_Exclusion` takes that and the highway volume's polygons (`Reroute_1`) as ONE clip union.
   Four pieces on the demo level, 6 178 033.8 m2.
5. **The scatter** -- `FillRandomly_0 Fill_Areas_Randomly` (`PointsPerSquaredMeter` = `TreeDensity`, every other
   setting the primitive default): `geo.ground.fill_random`, the sampler's world-aligned grid of one point per
   cell of `sqrt(1e4 / TreeDensity)` cm (447.21 cm at 0.05), jittered over the cell less 2 cm, kept inside the
   area; each point's box +-10 cm, steepness 1, a seed of Python's own. One point set per area that holds a point.
   -> `ForestPoints`.

### 2.2 Native: the texture writes

`Subgraph_12 Write_Attribute_fromTexture` (`$scale`, mask G, remapped to `ScaleMin`..`ScaleMax`,
`TextureTransform` scale 250 000) and `Subgraph_2` (`index`, mask G, remapped to 1..9 -- `CreateConstant_3`
on its `RemapValueOutHigh` -- scale 35 000), both reading `T_CloudNoise_4k`. Measured on the oracle: the output is
the input points with `$Scale` set to (s, s, s) and a Double `index` attribute added; location, density, seed and
everything else untouched.

### 2.3 `s18_outer_forest_trees` (after the texture writes)

6. **Species jitter** -- `AttributeNoise_2` (Add, `InputSource` `index`): + U(-0.5, 0.5) per point
   (`CreateConstant_2` 0.5, `AttributeMathsOp_5` x -1 on `NoiseMin`). Not clamped (only `$Density` is).
7. **Species normalisation** -- `FilterDataByType_5`, `MergePoints_0`, `AttributeReduce_0/1` (min / max of
   `index` over every set together), `AttributeRemap_1` (`bAllowInverseRange`): `index` from [min, max] to
   [0, 10] (`AttributeMathsOp_6` = `CreateConstant_3` 9 + 1, the default Add). So `Assign_Assets`' truncation gives
   entries 0..7 to [0, 8) and 8, 9, 10 name no entry -- kept with entry 0 (`geo.assets.index_picks`, stage 13).
8. **Projection** -- `Subgraph_10 Transform_Project` (ProjectPositions only), surface = the final mesh terrain:
   z = the surface height under the point; a miss keeps the point and its z (none on the demo).
9. **Water split** -- `FilterDataByType_1`, `AttributeFilter_0` (`$Position.Z`, constant threshold left at its
   default Double 0, operator default `Greater`): above water `z > 0`, below the rest (`Reroute_0`).
10. **Shore weight** -- `Subgraph_11 Write_DistanceFrom` (ComputeIn2D, MaximumDistance 5 000, Normalized,
    UseRemap 1 -> 0): every projected point's 2D distance to the nearest point ABOVE water (the targets merged,
    their centre the octree box centre -- `geo.distance`), capped at 5 000, / 5 000, remapped from the values'
    min..max over every set to 1..0. A point on land is 1, a point 50 m or more out to sea 0.
11. **Shore density** -- `CopyAttributes_0` (`@Last` -> `$density`, a float), `AttributeNoise_0` (Add,
    U(-0.3, 0.3): `CreateConstant_0` 0.3, `AttributeMathsOp_2` x -1), clamped to 0..1 (`PCGAttributeNoise.cpp:360`
    forces the clamp on `$Density`).
12. **Near the shore** -- `FilterDataByType_2`, `AttributeFilter_2`: density > 0.8.
13. **Deep-forest weight** -- `Subgraph_14 Write_DistanceFrom` (ComputeIn2D, MaximumDistance 10 000, Normalized,
    UseRemap 0 -> 1): distance to the nearest point BELOW water, likewise.
14. **Deep-forest density** -- `CopyAttributes_1`, `AttributeNoise_1` (U(-0.1, 0.1): `CreateConstant_1`,
    `AttributeMathsOp_4`), clamped; `AttributeRemap_0` (`$Density`, 0..1 -> 0.6..1; In range and Out max the
    defaults).
15. **Scale** -- `AttributeMathsOp_3`: `$Scale *= $Density`.
16. **Below-water cut** -- `FilterDataByType_3`, `AttributeFilter_3`: `$Position.Z > -450`.
17. **The trees** -- `Branch_0` (`UseMegaPlantsTrees`) -> `Subgraph_15` (eight Black Alder field meshes,
    `ScaleOffset` 0.7) or `Subgraph_18` (eight Megaplants): `Assign_Assets` with `UseIndexattribute` on `Index`,
    random rotation (+-2, +-2, +-180), random scale (1 +- 0.2, uniform) -- the same two calls as stage 13's
    `Subgraph_26` / `Subgraph_14`, so `s13_leftover_trees.FOREST` / `MEGA` and their settings are imported, not
    copied. The merged target set (`$Seed += dataIndex`) is `geo.assets.merge_targets` (moved there from stage 13's
    `upright`, which now calls it with the roll / pitch zeroing stage 13 has and stage 18 has not). Static meshes:
    the primitive's output is the moved targets alone. `Branch_1` (`Partitioned` false) -> the kept `SpawnForest_0`.

Surface: 67 official nodes, 47 computation by the brief; 42 are in Python (the named reroute and the reroutes
became edges), 5 stay native: the two texture writes, `CreateConstant_3` on `Subgraph_2`'s pin (its 9 is also
read in Python, step 7), and `FilterDataByTag_0` / `ExtractAttributeAtIndex_0`, which read a data-domain attribute
a Python node cannot (stage 10 keeps the same pair).

## 3. Deliberate differences

1. **New random numbers** (spec E2): the scatter's jitter and seeds, the three noise steps and the assignment's
   rotations and scales are Python's `ctx.rng`.
2. **The scatter's densities.** The engine's sampler gives each point the density `(Ratio - Chance) / Ratio`, a
   uniform random number; the port writes 1. Nothing reads it: the texture writes keep it (measured), and
   `CopyAttributes_0` overwrites it with the shore weight before any filter (step 11).
3. **Attributes nothing reads** are not reproduced: `ProjectAttribute` (0.0 on every point, written by
   `Transform_Project`) and the assignment's `dataIndex`. The spawner reads `Asset`.
4. **No partition**: `Branch_1`, `GetGraphParameter_1`, `Subgraph_17 GridPartition`, `Loop_0` dropped with
   `Partitioned`.

## 4. What could not be reproduced, and what was done instead

1. **The texture read stays native** (spec 4.3): pure Python cannot read the texture's pixels. With the official
   texture missing the port places 0 trees, as the official graph does. In a project that has the texture (or
   the town, if it gets one) the port takes the texture's values exactly as the official graph does, because
   the very same two nodes run.
2. **The artery width is read natively** (`FilterDataByTag_0`, `ExtractAttributeAtIndex_0`): a Python node cannot
   read a data-domain attribute in this build (stage 10).
3. **An empty target set for a distance step** (no point above water, or none below) is not on the demo level.
   Read from `PCGDistance.cpp:150-200`: with `bOutputToAttribute`, no target gives every source the maximum
   distance, so the remap's point range gives every point (out min + out max) / 2 = 0.5; `geo.distance` does
   that. Not measured.

## 5. Corrections to the brief

1. **"Upstream of the texture read: the exclusion areas, the scatter, the projection, and the water / shore /
   deep-forest decision"** -- the texture writes follow the scatter directly (`Subgraph_12 <- FillRandomly_0`);
   the projection and the water decisions are DOWNSTREAM and receive nothing officially. Measured on the oracle
   instead (section 0).
2. **"Subtract the highway and road occupation"** -- only the highway is subtracted. `Get Roads` feeds only the
   artery width (the city buffer); the road footprint is not a clip. On the demo level the road footprint lies
   inside the grown city but for one 20 193 m2 piece around the central park, which the park's 19 m buffer covers:
   the forest areas hold 0 m2 of road (6, `pyg_probe_t23_areas.py`), so "no tree on a road" holds there -- through
   the two buffers, not by construction.
3. **"The tree count matches density x available area within +-20 %"** -- false of the official graph: that is
   the SCATTER count (308 785 against density x area = 308 902, -0.04 %); the trees are what the water decisions
   keep, 34 659 of 308 785 (11.2 %) on the oracle -- most of the footprint is sea. Replaced by: the scatter count
   is density x available area within one point per grid cell the areas' boundaries cross (`checks/s18.
   count_allowance`, perimeter / cell + 5: 5 534 on the demo level; the grid holds one point per cell, so only
   a boundary cell can go either way), and every tree is a scattered point (projected).
4. **"Every scale is within [ScaleMin, ScaleMax]"** -- holds only at the texture write (oracle: 0.7389..1.9797).
   The tree's scale is that x the deep-forest density (0.6..1) x the assignment's `ScaleOffset` (0.7, Black
   Alder) x its random scale (0.8..1.2): within [0.6 x 0.7 x 0.8 x ScaleMin, 0.7 x 1.2 x ScaleMax] for the Black
   Alder trees (oracle 0.3515..1.3973), without the 0.7 for the Megaplants. Replaced by that bound.
5. **"A point's Z is its terrain height within 5 cm"** holds, exactly (the projection; the assignment moves no
   point). **"Empty terrain -> no trees"** holds: no terrain points, no footprint, no area, no scatter.
6. **"Python: ... species index and scale from the texture, jittered and normalised"** -- the scale is NOT
   jittered or normalised after the texture write; only the species index is (steps 6-7). The scale is scaled by
   the deep-forest density (steps 14-15).
