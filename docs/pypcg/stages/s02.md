## 2. The computation, as numbered steps

1. **The two squares** -- `Subgraph_4` and `Subgraph_5` (`Create_Spline_Shape`, overrides `Width` =
   `Length`; `Ngon` 4, `AtWorldOrigin` true and `Position`/`Rotation` zero by the primitive's own
   defaults). With `AtWorldOrigin` true the CoreProcess skips the volume transform (`Branch_4` B side),
   so both squares are centred on the WORLD origin with sides `OuterSize` and `CenterSize`; the
   volume's own transform (origin, scale 25/25/10) plays no part. `CreateConstant_0` is the centre
   size (step 1 above).
2. **The ring** -- `Exclude_Center` (`Compose_Areas_Exclusion`): its CoreProcess is one
   `Polygon2DOperation_0` `Difference` of the `Input:1` polygon from the `Input:0` polygon. In Python
   the ring is analytic: `|x| <= OuterSize/2 and |y| <= OuterSize/2` and not strictly inside the
   centre square (`on_ring`).
3. **The scatter** -- `ScatterPoints` (`Fill_Areas_Randomly`, overrides `PointsPerSquaredMeter` 1e-6,
   `RotationRange.Yaw` 180). Its CoreProcess runs `SurfaceSampler_1` (non-legacy grid,
   `PointExtents` 1, `Looseness` = 1 - `Uniformity` = 1, `bUnbounded`) over the ring as a surface.
   `FSurfaceSamplerData::Initialize` (PCGSurfaceSampler.cpp) makes the cell side
   `sqrt(1e4 / PointsPerSquaredMeter)` cm = **1 km** and the jitter square inside it `cell - 2` cm; cell
   indices run `ceil(min / cell) .. floor(max / cell)` over the ring's bounds, i.e. **-12 .. 12**; each
   cell draws a chance (`Ratio` is 1 on this path, so every cell is a candidate, and the chance becomes
   the density `1 - chance`), then X, then Y; a candidate off the ring is dropped. `AttributeMathsOp_8/
   10/11` lerp the rotation by `1 - SurfaceRotation` = 0 (no change), `AttributeMathsOp_0` sets roll 0,
   `TransformPoints_1` adds a yaw drawn in `[-Yaw, Yaw]` (offsets and scale ranges are zero), and
   `AttributeMathsOp_6` sets the extents to `OutputPointsExtents` (10, 10, 10). `sample()` is all of
   that.
4. **The cull** -- `Subgraph_6` (`Filter_RandomChoice`, `Ratio` 0.641598, `RandomPerDataset` false):
   its CoreProcess adds a `Random` attribute by `AttributeNoise_0` and keeps the points whose noise is
   at most `Ratio` (a Bernoulli draw per point). The port keeps exactly `round(n x KeepRatio)` points
   with `geo.choose` (the approved new randomness, spec E2).
5. **The spawn** -- kept: `SpawnAssets` reads the Python `Points`.

## 3. Deliberate differences

1. **New random numbers** (spec E2): the jitter, the yaws, the densities, the point seeds and the cull
   are Python's (`ctx.rng`), so the layout is new; V2 judges count, bounds and mesh shares over five
   seeds.
2. **The cull keeps an exact count** (`geo.choose`) where the official keeps a Bernoulli sample. The
   official count therefore varies by about +-12 points (sd of Binomial(583, 0.64)) around the same
   mean; the port's varies only with the edge cells.
3. **The point seed** is a fresh random int32 per point instead of the sampler's `FRandomStream` state;
   `Spawn_Assets` picks a mesh per point from it, so the share of each mesh stays about one third
   (V2: within 5 pp of the official on every mesh).
4. **The `Random` attribute** `Filter_RandomChoice` leaves on its output is not written: nothing reads
   it (`Spawn_Assets` picks by seed).
5. The Python output carries the tag `Points` instead of the primitives' `Type:`/`Primitive:` tags.
6. The kept `SpawnAssets` node loses its settings `Seed` (532 343 038), as every `keep()` does; its
   asset pick therefore draws other numbers. Statistical only.

## 4. What could not be reproduced, and the brief's count invariant

Nothing the contract asks for. One measured correction to the brief: **"the count equals round(ring
area x density x KeepRatio)" does not hold for the official sampler, and a port built that way fails
V2's bounds check.** The grid's cells -12 .. 12 cover X and Y from -12 km to +13 km, so the band
-12.5 .. -12 km is never sampled and the band +12.5 .. +13 km falls off the square: the reachable ring
is 24.5 km squared minus the centre, **583.3** points' worth, not the full ring's **608.0**. Measured
2026-10-19: the official output spans X -1 199 538 .. 1 248 371 and Y -1 197 847 .. 1 245 074 cm with
394 points; a uniform scatter over the whole square would reach -1 250 000, 500 m outside the official
bounds (V2's margin is 200 m). The port samples the same grid, so:

- `sampled` = the points that land on the ring, within 4 sd of `expected_sampled()` (583.3, sd
  `sqrt(sum p (1 - p))` = 3.62 over the cells; the 1 % bound is asserted on a 20-seed mean);
- the published count is exactly `round(sampled x KeepRatio)` (about 374);
- the stage records `sampled N` in its notes for every run.

The official run's 394 is +1.7 sd above that mean under its Bernoulli cull; V2's median 373 is -5.3 %
from it, inside the 25 % count limit.
